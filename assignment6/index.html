My Favorite Website

Google

Google is a major American technology company focused on search, AI, and online tools. Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, it is a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc.


Youtube

YouTube is a free online video-sharing platform owned by Google where users can watch, upload, and share video content.


w3schools

W3Schools is a popular free online learning website used to study coding, computer programming, and web development. Launched in 1998, its name comes from the World Wide Web (WWW), though it is not connected to the official World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).
